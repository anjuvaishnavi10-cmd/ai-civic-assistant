How AI Civic Assistant Works

A transparent three-step pipeline designed to accelerate civic incident resolution from street detection to department closure.

1

1. Citizen Reports

Citizens spot a civic problem (pothole, water burst, or garbage), capture a photo, tag their location, and submit the complaint within seconds.

2

2. AI Vision Analysis

Computer vision scans the uploaded image to classify the civic category, estimate severity, assign priority, and eliminate duplicate or spam submissions.

3

3. Action & Resolution

The municipal ward crew receives targeted alerts, dispatches repair teams, and updates the three-stage status tracker from Pending to In Progress to Resolved.
